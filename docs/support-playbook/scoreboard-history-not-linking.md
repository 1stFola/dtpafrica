# ScoreBoard History Not Linking

Tier: Tier 2 - AI-assisted human resolution

## Symptoms

- Player appears in match timeline but not on their profile.
- Opponent or secondary player mention is missed.
- Single-name timeline entry is not clickable.

## Diagnose

- Check `playerLabel`, `playerLabel2`, and `assistLabel`.
- Confirm the player has a complete name.
- Review duplicate candidates in Name QA.

## Recommendation

Complete names before linking. Merge duplicate identities only after human review.
